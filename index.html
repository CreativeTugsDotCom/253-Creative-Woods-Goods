Our Story

A Little Wood.
A Lot of Heart.

We're a family-run business based in DuPont, Washington, deeply passionate about creating beautiful, functional pieces from locally sourced wood.

Mario and the family pour care and attention into every cut, sand, and finish. We believe that everyday objects should have character, telling a story of craftsmanship and dedication that mass-produced items simply cannot match.
